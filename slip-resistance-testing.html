Common Questions

Questions about this service.

Plain-language answers to the questions buyers commonly ask before booking.

01What is slip resistance testing?+

Slip resistance testing measures the frictional characteristics of pedestrian surfaces under specified test conditions. Prime Safety provides on-site wet pendulum and dry floor friction testing for existing pedestrian surfaces in accordance with AS 4663:2013.

02What is the difference between wet and dry testing?+

Wet pendulum testing assesses slip resistance under specified wet conditions. Dry floor friction testing using the Tortus 3 assesses a surface under specified dry conditions. One method does not automatically replace the other.

03Which Australian Standard applies to existing floors?+

AS 4663:2013 applies to the on-site measurement of existing pedestrian surfaces. AS 4586 relates primarily to the classification of new pedestrian surface materials.

04Is Prime Safety NATA accredited?+

Prime Safety is an independent testing provider and is not itself a NATA-accredited laboratory. Our wet and dry slip-testing equipment is calibrated by ISTS, a NATA-accredited laboratory.
