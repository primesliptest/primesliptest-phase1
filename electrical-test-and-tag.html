Electrical Test & Tag

Testing, tagging and documented records.

Prime Safety inspects and tests in-service electrical equipment within the agreed scope, then identifies outcomes through tagging and structured records. Failed equipment is documented and handled in line with the approved failure wording.

01

Inspection

Visual + applicable tests
In-service inspection and testing within agreed scope.
AS/NZS 3760:2022

What the scope can cover

  • Performed by a trained competent person.
  • Records can support safety, maintenance and risk-management purposes.
  • Failed equipment is tagged FAILED — DO NOT USE and isolated from use where practicable.
  • Repairs are referred to an appropriately licensed electrical worker where required.
  • Scope and timing are confirmed before site attendance.
Common Questions

Questions about this service.

Plain-language answers to the questions buyers commonly ask before booking.

01How long does Test & Tag take?+

Timing depends on the number and type of appliances. A time estimate can be provided once the site scope is understood.

02What happens to failed equipment?+

Failed equipment is identified and tagged FAILED — DO NOT USE, isolated from use where practicable and reported to the site representative.

03Can you provide asset-register information?+

Yes. Asset-register documentation can form part of the agreed testing scope.
