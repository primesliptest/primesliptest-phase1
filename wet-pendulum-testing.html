Wet Pendulum Testing

Measure wet slip resistance in the field.

Wet pendulum testing assesses the frictional characteristics of an existing pedestrian surface under specified wet conditions. Prime Safety sets out the agreed test locations, completes the field testing and documents the measured results and applicable classifications in the report.

01

Wet Pendulum

WammaLab Pendulum
On-site wet slip resistance measurement for existing pedestrian surfaces.
AS 4663:2013

Standards distinction: AS 4663:2013 covers slip resistance measurement of existing pedestrian surfaces. AS 4586:2013 covers slip resistance classification of new pedestrian surface materials.

What the scope can cover

  • Suitable hard pedestrian surfaces are assessed subject to method and site suitability.
  • The classification considered appropriate depends on location, intended use and applicable guidance.
  • P0 to P5 are wet pendulum slip-resistance classifications.
  • Wet and dry testing are different methods and should not be treated as interchangeable.
  • Calibration wording: equipment calibrated by ISTS, a NATA-accredited laboratory.
Common Questions

Questions about this service.

Plain-language answers to the questions buyers commonly ask before booking.

01What does P-classification mean?+

P0 to P5 are wet pendulum slip-resistance classifications, with a higher classification generally indicating greater slip resistance under the specified wet test conditions.

02Can wet testing be completed while a site is operating?+

In many cases, yes. Access is coordinated with the site representative and work is planned to minimise disruption. Temporary control of the immediate test area may be required.

03How quickly is the report issued?+

Professional PDF reports are normally issued within the approved 24–48-hour timeframe after the site inspection.
