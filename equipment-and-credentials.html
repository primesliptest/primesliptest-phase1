What the standards mean in practice

01

AS 4663:2013

Provides methods for measuring the frictional characteristics of existing pedestrian surfaces in wet and dry conditions.

02

AS 4586:2013

Provides means of classifying new pedestrian surface materials according to their frictional characteristics.

03

SA HB 198:2014

Guidance used alongside the Australian slip-resistance standards when specifying and interpreting pedestrian surface performance.

04

Electrical Compliance

AS/NZS 3760:2022 is applied to the agreed electrical Test & Tag scope, with electrical repair work referred to an appropriately licensed electrical worker.
