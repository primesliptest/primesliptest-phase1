Combined Wet + Dry Testing

One site. Two conditions.

Wet and dry slip testing measure different conditions and produce different results. A combined scope can be useful where the property, surface use or risk-management process requires evidence under both wet and dry conditions.

01

Wet condition

WammaLab Pendulum
Wet slip resistance under specified conditions.
AS 4663:2013

02

Dry condition

Tortus 3
Dry floor friction under specified conditions.
AS 4663:2013

Standards distinction: AS 4663:2013 covers slip resistance measurement of existing pedestrian surfaces. AS 4586:2013 covers slip resistance classification of new pedestrian surface materials.

What the scope can cover

  • The scope is agreed before site attendance.
  • Wet and dry results remain distinct; one method does not automatically replace the other.
  • Selected locations can be tested under the appropriate condition.
  • Equipment is calibrated by ISTS, a NATA-accredited laboratory.
  • Combined testing can support broader evidence for safety and risk-management decisions.
Common Questions

Questions about this service.

Plain-language answers to the questions buyers commonly ask before booking.

01Why combine wet and dry testing?+

Because wet pendulum and dry floor friction testing assess different conditions. A combined scope provides measurements under both where both are relevant.

02Can every floor be tested using both methods?+

No. The appropriate method depends on surface, location, intended use and site suitability. Prime Safety confirms the scope before testing.
