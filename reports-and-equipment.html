What the report can include

  • Site and test location identification.
  • Surface description, test method and relevant test conditions.
  • Equipment used and measured results.
  • Applicable classifications where relevant.
  • Photographs and relevant observations.
  • Practical next-step recommendations where included in the agreed scope.
