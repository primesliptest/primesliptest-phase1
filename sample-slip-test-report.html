Methodology & conditions

The report structure follows the source report’s distinction between wet pendulum testing and dry floor friction testing for existing pedestrian surfaces.

WET

Wet Pendulum

Measures frictional characteristics under wet conditions using a pendulum-mounted rubber slider. Results are expressed as BPN/SRV and classified using the applicable wet classification guidance.

Standard
AS 4663:2013, Appendix A
Typical slider
Rubber Slider 96 (4S) for smoother indoor surfaces
Output
Mean BPN / wet classification
DRY

Dry Floor Friction

Measures the coefficient of friction of an existing surface under dry conditions using a floor friction tester at a controlled speed.

Standard
AS 4663:2013 dry floor friction method
Equipment
Tortus 3 / comparable floor friction tester
Output
Mean CoF / D classification
Important: dry friction results describe dry-condition performance and do not replace wet-condition assessment where wet or contaminated conditions are relevant.

Classification guides

The underlying report uses AS 4586:2013 classification guidance for classification examples, with SA HB 198:2014 interpretative guidance. For existing pedestrian surfaces, AS 4663:2013 covers slip resistance measurement; AS 4586:2013 covers classification of new pedestrian surface materials. The examples below are presented as an awareness aid, not a substitute for a project-specific assessment.
